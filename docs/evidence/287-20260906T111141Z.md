## The finding, restated in one sentence

> **A synthetic name is never a baseline member.**

Every citation fixture was synthetic — `references/a.md`, `references/over0.md`.
No synthetic name is in `BASELINE_REFERENCES`, so **no fixture could exercise
the exemption at all**. Cases 12 and 13 of `docs/test-plans/35-plan.md` pass
under the broken cap and the fixed one alike. The tests were green while the
rule they guarded admitted only skills with no dependencies of their own.

## What changed

Fixtures are sliced from the roster **read from disk at run time**, never
restated — the case-1 pattern of `docs/test-plans/35-plan.md`.

Three guards keep them honest, and each fails loudly rather than degrading:

| Guard | Prevents |
|---|---|
| The baseline is a **subset** of the roster | the exemption being untestable because the two populations diverged |
| The discretionary population **exceeds the cap** | an over-cap fixture ceasing to be constructible from real names, which is how a suite slides back to synthetic ones |
| Every path the builders emit **exists on disk** | a synthetic name reappearing by hand |

rule with exactly one carve-out, and the carve-out has a reason on the record.
