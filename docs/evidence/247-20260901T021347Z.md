## Case 10 — what the pass condition actually demands

The threshold is not *"a dependency is recorded"*. It is that the entry **names
the capability M5 cannot deliver without a named local model**. A note reading
"no local model is chosen yet" would satisfy the first and fail the second.

The entry therefore partitions the milestone rather than describing the gap. It
lands in `## Dependencies and sequencing`, with a pointer in the milestone header
so a reader meets it before the stories.

## S5.4 is the finding worth carrying forward

S5.4 is the only story split by the line. Its emergency short-circuit, brief
structure and linter ship; a brief whose prose is generated does not.

Left implicit, that becomes a story that looks deliverable, gets planned as a
whole, and discovers its blocker mid-flight. The entry therefore requires S5.4's
Design to state **which side it took** — template-rendered and revisited later,
or waiting for a model. Inferring that choice from silence is the failure mode
the entry exists to prevent, and it is the same structural trap that stranded
#14's follow-ups and #235.

## What this task deliberately does not do

It does not name a model. Decision 3 of `docs/design/stories/243.md` places that
outside the Story: it is a procurement and privacy decision belonging to whoever
owns M5, and choosing one here would be an agent authoring a decision it has no
standing to take. The entry records the absence, its consequences, and that
naming a model is itself unscoped work M5 owns.
